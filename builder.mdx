## Overview

The **Quotes** module is the commercial proposal engine of A5SYNC. It allows your sales team to turn customer interest into accurate, approved, professional proposals and convert them into sales orders.

### The lifecycle of a quote

A quote moves through the system in five distinct stages:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inquiry">
    A request arrives via a web form (landing in **Requests**) or via direct contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Creation">
    You build the quote in **Builder**, adding line items and customer details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    If the quote triggers a rule defined in **Configurations**, it enters the **Approval** state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer Action">
    The customer reviews the quote styled by your **Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order Conversion">
    Once accepted, you click **Convert to Order** to generate a **Sales Order**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  **Pro Tip:** Actions in Quotes have downstream consequences. An approved quote can trigger an order, which triggers an invoice, impacting your revenue reporting.
</Info>

## title: "Builder" description: "The operational heart for creating, editing, and finalizing quotes."

The **Builder** is the operational heart of the module—where quotes are created, edited, reviewed, and finalized.

**When to use it:** Every time you start a new proposal, update an existing one, or push a finalized quote into an order.

### Find a Quote

Open **Quotes \> Builder** in the sidebar. The list shows every quote in the system.

| Tool | What it does |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Status tabs** | Filter by lifecycle stage (Draft, Approval, Ready, etc.) |
| **My Quotes Only** | Toggle to see only quotes you own |
| **Search quotes...** | Search by quote title or ID |
| **All Filters** | Apply advanced filtering |
| **Sort By** | Reorder the list |

---

### Create a Quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start">
    Click **\+ Create Quote** in the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify">
    Enter a **Quote Title** in the modal. Title is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initialize">
    Click **Create New Quote**. The system assigns a unique ID and sets the status to **Draft**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Build a Complete Quote

The editor is divided into sections. Recommended workflow:

1. **Set the Customer**: Always start here. The customer affects tax, shipping, and approval logic.
2. **Add Shipping Information**: Enter recipient details to update the Summary panel.
3. **Choose Payment Method**: Select from the dropdown (Card, Check, etc.).
4. **Add Line Items**: Search by product name or MPN. Adjust quantity or margin as needed.

<Note>
  A quote with no customer cannot be approved or converted. This is the step easiest to skip but most likely to cause issues later.
</Note>

## Quote Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Draft** | Still being assembled; not yet submitted |
| **Approval** | Waiting on a reviewer to act |
| **Ready** | Cleared review and ready to send |
| **Ordered** | Converted into a sales order |
| **Expired** | Passed its validity date |

## Configurations

If Builder is where work happens, Configurations is where the rules are defined.

### Approval Rules

Approval rules route quotes to a reviewer based on specific risk thresholds (e.g., low margins).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Toggle **Enable Quote Approval** to ON.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define">
    Click **\+ Add New Rule** and set your Field, Operator, and Value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Rules** to apply changes to all new quotes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you enable **Automatic Order Creation**, approved quotes flow directly into Sales Orders without manual intervention.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Tax is showing $0.00">
    Check **Customers \> Companies**. The entity is likely marked as approved tax-exempt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't click 'Connect to Deal'">
    Ensure the quote has a linked customer, at least one line item, a payment method, and has cleared the approval queue.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A quote disappeared from my list">
    It may have been reassigned. Check **Transfer Requests** to see if ownership was shifted to another rep.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
